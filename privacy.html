Privacy Policy

DraftBox is built to help freelancers draft client-facing documents faster. This page explains, in plain language, what data the current app stores and how it is used.

Last updated: May 6, 2026

What We Collect

The app stores the account details you provide at signup, the plan you select, and the document metadata needed to show your dashboard history. During local demo use, some document state is also stored in your browser's local storage.

How It Is Used

Your information is used to sign you in, track your selected plan, and generate or save document drafts. If an AI API key is configured on the server, document details may be sent to that provider so DraftBox can generate content for you.

Storage

This build uses a simple file-backed datastore on the server and browser local storage on the client. It is suitable for prototype use and internal testing, but it is not intended to be treated as a hardened production deployment.

Your Choices

You can stop using the app at any time, clear your local browser data, or contact the site owner to remove server-side records from the prototype datastore.
