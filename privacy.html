2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

We do not use your information to send unsolicited marketing communications, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Document Input and AI Processing

When you generate a document, the information you enter into the form — including business names, project descriptions, financial figures, and other details — is transmitted to Anthropic's API servers to produce the AI-generated output. Do not enter sensitive personal information (such as Social Security numbers, bank account numbers, or government ID numbers) into document generation forms. Anthropic's data handling practices are governed by their privacy policy linked above.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account information and generated documents for as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required by law to retain it longer. Anonymized or aggregated data that cannot identify you may be retained indefinitely for analytics purposes.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser localStorage to store your session token and to cache your document list locally on your device for performance. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies. Standard server logs may capture your IP address and browser information as part of normal web operations.

7. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS), encrypted database storage, and hashed passwords. However, no system is completely secure. We cannot guarantee the absolute security of your information, and we encourage you to use a strong, unique password and to not share your login credentials.

9. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information we collect and how it is used, the right to request deletion of your personal information, and the right to opt out of the sale of your personal information. We do not sell personal information. To exercise your CCPA rights, contact us at legal@draftbox.app.

If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 18, we will delete it promptly. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately at legal@draftbox.app.
